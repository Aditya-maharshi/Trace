## 2024-05-18 - Missing ARIA Labels on Icon Buttons\n**Learning:** The `CommercialConsole.tsx` component had an icon-only button (✕) for closing the Trace AI panel that lacked an `aria-label`, making it inaccessible to screen readers.\n**Action:** Always verify that buttons containing only icons or visual symbols (like "✕") have descriptive `aria-label` attributes to ensure they are properly announced by assistive technologies.
