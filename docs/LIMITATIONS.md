# Methodology & Limitations Disclosure

**Project:** VASP Attribution & Sanctions Screening Engine  
**Deployment Target:** Ethereum Mainnet  
**Last Updated:** September 2026  

---

## 1. Investigative Lead Notice & Regulatory Disclaimer

> [!WARNING]
> **This software produces automated investigative leads, NOT definitive legal or compliance determinations.**
>
> A "LOW" risk score or absence of VASP attribution does **NOT** certify that a wallet is clean, unassociated with illicit activity, or exempt from regulatory reporting obligations. All attributions, path scores, and risk tiers are generated by automated heuristics and **require qualified human compliance review** prior to filing Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs), freezing assets, or executing regulatory actions.

---

## 2. VASP Registry Coverage & Boundaries

- **Fixed Public Registry:** The tool identifies Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs) by matching against a curated registry of **366 publicly-known exchange deposit and hot wallet addresses** (maintained in `data/vasp_labels.json` and sourced from open-source intelligence).
- **Non-Exhaustive Scope:** This is **not** a commercial attribution dataset (such as Chainalysis, Elliptic, or TRM Labs).
- **Blind Spots:** Private OTC desks, regional or unlisted exchanges, newly generated deposit addresses, and decentralized dark pools not present in the registry will not be identified as VASPs.

---

## 3. Sanctions Screening Boundaries

- **Data Source:** Sanctions screening is powered by the **OpenSanctions Community Free-Tier API** (`api.opensanctions.org`).
- **Lists Covered:** Major global watchlists including US OFAC (SDN, non-SDN), EU Consolidated, UN Security Council, and UK HMT.
- **Latency & Coverage Gaps:** While community feeds are frequently updated, free-tier endpoints may exhibit update latency compared to direct paid commercial feeds. Unlisted proxy wallets, newly designated entities, or addresses designated within the preceding hours may produce false negatives until indexed.

## 4. Confidence Threshold Calibration (Empirical Protocol)

Confidence mapping (`toConfidence()` in `lib/attribution.ts`) is based on an empirical offline calibration run performed in **September 2026** using `scripts/calibrate.ts`:

- **Sample Size:** 50 real active known-VASP-linked wallets + 50 random baseline Ethereum wallets.
- **Methodology:**
  - **High Confidence:** Calibrated against the 90th percentile of known-VASP-linked scores (`12.01`). Active threshold set conservatively at `> 8.0` with human review required for updates.
  - **Low Confidence:** Calibrated against the baseline of random non-VASP wallets (median `0.00`, 90th percentile `9.43`).
- **Re-calibration:** The calibration script is maintained at [`scripts/calibrate.ts`](file:///c:/Users/harsh/OneDrive/Desktop/SIH/Backend_1/scripts/calibrate.ts) and can be re-executed anytime via `npm run calibrate`.

Capital flight and exchange settlement overwhelmingly use the 5 tokens listed above. Unlisted token transfers are not traversed.

## 6. Structuring Signal Detection Caveats

The graph builder includes a secondary ranking heuristic (`rankNeighborsByStructuringSignal` in `lib/graphBuilder.ts`) designed to prioritize neighbors showing indicators of structuring:
- High transaction frequency (splitting funds into multiple transfers).
- Low value variance (uniform amounts near reporting thresholds).
- Tight time clustering (transfers grouped within short intervals).

**Caveat:** While this heuristic improves traversal through structured split-hops, **it does not make traversal exhaustive**. Complex laundering schemes involving multi-week peel chains, nested mixers, smart-contract routers, or off-chain coordinated transfers remain difficult to trace via bounded BFS.

---

## 7. Cross-Chain Bridge Boundaries

- **Single-Chain Horizon:** This engine traces transactions exclusively on **Ethereum Mainnet**.
- **Bridge Exit Detection:** Traversal actively checks against an audited registry of major cross-chain bridge contracts (Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, BSC, Avalanche, Solana Wormhole, etc.).
- When transaction flow terminates at a bridge contract:
  - The system flags `traceExitedToBridge: true` with bridge metadata.
  - **The trace stops at the bridge exit point.**
  - The result is **inconclusive**, not clean. Multi-chain investigation tools must be used to follow funds across the destination chain.
